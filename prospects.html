How to use this

Makers made a product that ranked in the window, those flagged Top 3 made a genuine hit. Tick a row once you have reached someone. Use the To reach out and Reached out filters to see who is left in each list. The state is saved in this browser. The top 50 of the last 30 days are already ticked.
