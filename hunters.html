Read of it

Chris Messina and Rohan Chaubey are the two obvious names if we ever want an outside hunter with real reach. Messina is the platform's most famous hunter; Chaubey hunts AI and dev tools almost daily, which is exactly our audience. Garry Tan only hunts YC-connected launches, so realistic only if that applies. Most other top products were self-hunted, which is a reminder that self-hunting is completely normal at the top and an outside hunter is a bonus, not a requirement.
