Read of it

The three-month view makes the answer obvious. Rohan Chaubey hunted 25 of 92 daily #1s, a quarter of every single day, almost all of them AI and dev tools, which is exactly our audience. He is the standout target for an outside hunter. Ben Lang and Chris Messina tie next at 12 each; Messina is the platform's most famous hunter, Ben Lang is the ex-Notion community lead with deep reach. Garry Tan (8) only hunts YC-connected launches, so realistic only if that applies to us.

That said, plenty of #1s were self-hunted, so self-hunting is completely normal at the top and an outside hunter is a bonus, not a requirement. If we do pursue one, Chaubey is the clear first ask.
