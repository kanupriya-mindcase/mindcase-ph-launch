Read of it

Two hunters dominate the year: Rohan Chaubey (53 daily #1s) and Chris Messina (49) posted the #1 product on more than a quarter of all days between them. Behind them, fmerian (31), Zac Zuo (28), Garry Tan (26), and Ben Lang (24) complete a top six that hunted well over half of the year's #1s.

For Mindcase, Rohan Chaubey is the clear first ask: the most prolific by far, and almost entirely AI and dev tools, which is our exact audience. Chris Messina is next for sheer reach, with fmerian and Ben Lang as strong follow-ups. Garry Tan is realistic only through Y Combinator. And note the long tail: dozens of hunters landed just one #1 all year, a reminder that self-hunting is completely normal at the top and an outside hunter is a bonus, not a requirement.
