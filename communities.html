Communities

The places we can genuinely notify on launch day — without breaking PH's "no vote-begging" rule. The play: be a real, active member before launch, so a simple "we're live" lands with people who already know us.

Rule of thumb: earn the right to post by contributing for weeks first. A cold "please upvote" in a channel you never use gets ignored — or gets you muted.

Checklist

  • Join 3–4 relevant communitiesPick where our real users hang out, not just founder echo chambers.
  • Be genuinely active for 2+ weeksAnswer questions, share progress — build recognition before you need it.
  • Line up 15–20 first-hour supportersReal contacts who'll comment + upvote early. Personal DMs, never a vote-swap group.
  • Avoid a coordinated footprintDon't have every team account pile onto the same threads in the same way.
