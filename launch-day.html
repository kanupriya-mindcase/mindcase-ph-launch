Launch Day Playbook

The 24-hour run of show. PH's day resets at 12:01 AM PT (12:31 PM IST). You compete only against products launched the same day — the goal is Top 5, ideally #1. Engagement depth (comments, replies) matters as much as raw votes.

Proposed date: Wed, Aug 26. Lock it once assets are ready and you've checked the day is clear of big competing launches.

Run of show

  • 12:01 AM PT
    Go live + founder first comment
    Publish immediately. Kritish drops the origin-story comment right away.
  • First hour
    Wave 1 — close supporters
    Personal DMs to the 15–20. Early velocity is what the ranking rewards.
  • ~7 AM PT
    Wave 2 — email + X
    Notify the list and post the founder thread.
  • ~12 PM PT
    Wave 3 — LinkedIn + status
    "We're #X, thank you" keeps momentum visible.
  • ~3 PM PT
    Wave 4 — communities
    Final push where you've been active for weeks.
  • All 24h
    Reply to every comment, fast
    Ask questions back. Depth of discussion is ranking fuel.

Launch-day checklist

  • Published at 12:01 AM PT
  • Founder first comment posted
  • All co-founders tagged as makersSo the whole team can reply and share.
  • Notification waves sent on schedule
  • Replying to every comment within minutes
  • 30%-off landing page live + tracking on
