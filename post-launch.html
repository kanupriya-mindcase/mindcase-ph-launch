After the Launch

The launch spikes, then the long tail begins. This is where you lock in the durable value — the backlink, the social proof, and the momentum you can point to for the next thing.

Checklist

  • Add the PH badge to the sitePermanent, high-authority backlink — real SEO value.
  • Thank supporters publiclyReply to commenters, shout out the people who showed up.
  • Publish a "we launched" recapNumbers, lessons, screenshots. Repurpose across X + LinkedIn + blog.
  • Measure signups from the launchCheck GA4 for producthunt / referral — how many signed up, how many took the offer.
  • Follow up with new signupsOnboard the PH cohort well — activation is the real goal, not the badge.
  • Keep the relationshipsThe makers and supporters you met are worth more than the ranking. Stay in touch.
The badge and #1 are nice. The signups, the backlink, and the network are what actually compound.
