Audience & Activation

Our owned channels — email list + social — are the engine on launch day. The job now: grow coming-soon followers, prep notification copy, and set up tracking. On the day we notify in waves, never all at once, to keep upvote velocity steady across 24h.

Language matters: "we're live on Product Hunt, come see" — never "please upvote." Direct vote-begging gets penalized.
