Launch Assets

Everything shown on the PH post and the landing page. Draft in weeks 1–2, lock by week 3 so launch week is only execution. Write for the user, not the system — "get any data on the web," not "web-data extraction platform."

The asset list

  • Tagline (≤60 char)What Mindcase does for the user, in plain words. Clear beats clever.
  • Gallery — 5–8 imagesShow the product doing real work (LinkedIn, Maps, Amazon pulls), not marketing fluff.
  • Demo video / GIF (30–60s)The single highest-converting asset. Show a real extraction end to end.
  • Thumbnail + logoCrisp, on-brand, readable at small size in the feed.
  • Founder first commentKritish's origin story: why we built it, who it's for. Posted the second we go live.
  • "Coming Soon" page on PHPublish early — followers get auto-notified on launch day. Biggest single lever.
  • PH landing page + 30%-off offerPrototype already built (honest scarcity counter). Wire real copy + tracking.

The launch-day offer

30% off · first 100 signups

Rewards PH visitors, creates honest urgency, and gives us a clean conversion event to measure. Counter reflects real signups — never a fake auto-ticking number.

Tagline test: read it to someone outside the company. If they can't say what Mindcase does back to you, rewrite it.
